# BEO Art Studio Email Marketing Plan

**Audience:** 122 existing BEO AI Tools sign-ups  
**Campaign window:** 17 July–15 December 2026  
**Cadence:** 2 emails during the first 10 days, then 3 weekly (64 sends)  
**Primary business journey:** Try the tool → create and save art → buy credits → commission real artwork or a mural → use the client workspace

## The strategic idea

BEO should not market the AI tools and the physical art studio as separate businesses. The tools are the discovery and visualization layer; BEO Art Studio is the human craft that brings the best ideas into the real world.

Each week follows a dependable rhythm:

- **Tuesday — Teach:** a useful tool tip, creative prompt, or practical workflow.
- **Thursday — Inspire:** an artistic idea, style spotlight, transformation, studio story, or use case.
- **Saturday — Convert:** credits, referral, commission consultation, mural enquiry, artwork offer, or workspace offer.

For the first two weeks, send only to the full list twice weekly. From 28 July onward, send Tuesday and Thursday to active/marketable subscribers and reserve Saturday for people who opened or clicked in the previous 30 days, generated artwork, visited a tool, or have previously purchased. This keeps the program heavy without exhausting cold subscribers.

## Segments to create before launch

1. **New / never generated:** signed up but no completed generation. Goal: first creation.
2. **Tool user:** generated at least once, but has not purchased credits. Goal: repeat use and first credit purchase.
3. **Credit buyer:** has purchased a pack. Goal: repeat purchase, referral, commission, or workspace.
4. **Sketch / portrait intent:** clicked Photo to Sketch, pencil, charcoal, painting, or commission links. Goal: real drawing or painting enquiry.
5. **Mural intent:** used or clicked Mural Visualizer or mural booking links. Goal: mural consultation.
6. **Creator / studio:** opened Client Work, created a project, or clicked workspace plans. Goal: Creator or Studio Workspace.
7. **Engaged 30 days:** opened/clicked recently. Goal: receive the third weekly send.
8. **Cooling:** no opens for 30 days. Goal: reduce to one weekly send.
9. **Sunset:** no opens/clicks for 60 days. Goal: re-permission sequence, then suppress.

## Offer ladder

Use the lowest-friction appropriate offer instead of asking everyone to buy immediately.

| Stage | Offer | Best audience | Success event |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | First free creation | New / never generated | Completed first generation |
| Explore | Photo to Sketch or Mural Visualizer tutorial | All non-buyers | Tool visit / generation |
| Retain | My Generations gallery | Existing users | Return visit / download |
| Monetize | 5 credits for ₦2,000 | Light users | First purchase |
| Expand | 20 credits for ₦6,000 | Active creators | Creator pack purchase |
| Scale | 50 credits for ₦12,000 | Studios / heavy users | Studio pack purchase |
| Refer | Give one bonus credit, get one bonus credit after verified first purchase | Credit buyers / advocates | Qualified referral |
| Human service | Custom pencil drawing, charcoal portrait or full painting | Sketch / portrait intent | Quote request |
| High value | Mural consultation and painting | Mural / business intent | Qualified consultation |
| Professional | Free first client project | Artists / creators | First project created |
| Subscription | Creator Workspace: ₦6,000/month; Studio Workspace: ₦15,000/month | Active professionals | Paid workspace |

Promotional discounts, free shipping, deposits, deadlines and “limited slots” must only be used when BEO has approved the economics and the capacity claim is true. The calendar deliberately uses value, usefulness and real availability instead of fake urgency.

## Reusable email structure

Keep most emails between 100 and 220 words.

1. **One-line hook:** a specific creative possibility or problem.
2. **One visual:** preferably a BEO before/after, finished physical piece, mural mockup, or studio process photo.
3. **Three short points:** a tip, mini process, or why it matters.
4. **One primary CTA:** never make four buttons compete.
5. **Human sign-off:** “BEO Art Studio — real artists who paint for real.”
6. **Footer:** why the person is receiving the email, studio contact, physical/business identification where applicable, and a working one-click unsubscribe.

## Recommended automation alongside the calendar

These are behaviour-triggered and should pause the regular promotional email on the day they fire.

- **Welcome:** immediately after signup; one clear creation CTA.
- **No first generation:** 24 hours after signup; three-minute walkthrough.
- **First generation complete:** immediately; save/download, try another style, and show commission option.
- **Credits low or zero:** explain balance and the most suitable pack; no more than once every seven days.
- **Mural generated:** show how to refine the preview and invite a real mural enquiry.
- **Commission click, no enquiry:** 24 hours later; plain-text “send us the photo, size and city” follow-up.
- **First credit purchase:** receipt/thank-you plus three project ideas; referral introduced after value is received.
- **Project created, not shared:** 48 hours later; checklist for completing and sharing the proposal.
- **Proposal viewed:** notify creator and suggest a polite follow-up, if the product supports it.
- **Inactive 30 days:** one weekly digest only.
- **Inactive 60 days:** two-email re-permission sequence, then suppression.

## Measurement plan for a list of 122

At this size, raw percentages swing easily. Track both rates and actual people.

| Metric | Working benchmark / decision rule |
|---|---|
| Delivery | Aim for 98%+; investigate immediately below 97% |
| Unique open rate | Directional only because privacy systems inflate opens; use clicks and conversions for decisions |
| Unique click rate | Initial working goal: 3–6%; compare tutorial, inspiration and offer sends |
| Unsubscribe rate | Keep below 0.5% per send; if two sends exceed this, reduce frequency for cooling subscribers |
| Spam complaints | Target zero; stop the campaign and investigate any complaint spike |
| First-generation conversion | Track unique people completing their first creation within 72 hours of an email |
| Credit purchase conversion | Track buyer count, pack, and revenue by email/segment |
| Commission leads | Count qualified enquiries with photo/idea, size, city/location and timing |
| Mural leads | Count qualified enquiries with wall photo, dimensions/location and intended use |
| Workspace activation | Projects created, proposals shared, and approvals received |

Use tagged links such as `utm_source=email`, `utm_medium=lifecycle`, `utm_campaign=beo_2026_07_onboarding`, and a unique `utm_content` for each CTA. Do not rely on opens alone to define engagement.

## First-week launch checklist

- Confirm all 122 people explicitly signed up for marketing or that the signup wording supports these messages.
- Authenticate the sending domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
- Remove obvious test, duplicate and malformed addresses; never buy or append contacts.
- Create the segments above from Supabase events and user activity.
- Confirm unsubscribe and preference-centre links work on mobile.
- Use a recognisable sender name such as **BEO Art Studio** and a monitored reply-to address.
- Prepare at least four strong BEO-owned visuals: six-style comparison, wall-to-mural preview, digital-to-physical commission, and proposal workflow.
- Test every CTA, including login redirects and mobile layout.
- Send first to the most recently active 20–30 contacts, then the remainder if delivery is clean.
